Synopsis

Weakened by age and feeling death approach, Gaudí invites his new assistants to discover his studio to understand his vision and carry forward his dream. Stepping into the shoes of a disciple and guided by Gaudí's own voice, visitors discover the architect's extraordinary personality, revolutionary working method and rich creative universe.

With one goal: to understand Gaudí and, when the time comes, take over the masterpiece to which he devoted his life: the Sagrada Familia.

With Gaudí, The Divine Workshop, we want to take visitors inside the mind of the famous architect: this exceptional reconstruction of the studio destroyed in 1926 offers a narrative journey into the Catalan master's unique psyche.
